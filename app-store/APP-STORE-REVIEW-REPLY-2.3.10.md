# App Store Connect reply (iOS 1.0.0 - Guideline 2.3.10)

Copy into Resolution Center after uploading the revised screenshots.

Hi,

Thanks for the note on Guideline 2.3.10.

Updated screenshots are uploaded for all required sizes (including via View All Sizes in Media Manager). They highlight the main features of the app: sign-in, GPS clock in/out, today’s plan, tasks, team activity, and inbox.

Best regards,
BaseCrew team

---

## What you need to do

1. In App Store Connect → your iOS app → Previews and Screenshots, open **View All Sizes in Media Manager**.
2. Replace every size with the new files from `mobile-app/app-store/`:
   - Prefer `basecrew-asc-*-1290x2796.png` first, then `1242x2688` and `1179x2556`.
3. Paste the reply above into Resolution Center and submit for review again.
4. No new binary is required for this metadata-only issue unless Apple asks for one.
